About

Hi! I'm Aditya Srivastava, a Software Development Engineer at Amazon Web Services in Seattle. I build and harden high-throughput distributed services — fixing race conditions in production, migrating authentication across services, and shipping global launches with zero downtime.


I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from the University of Southern California (4.0 GPA, Honors). My sweet spot is where distributed systems meet machine learning: reliable infrastructure underneath, intelligent products on top — from a RAG-based onboarding assistant at AWS to vision transformers for document AI at Scry AI.


I'm also an open-source person at heart: I founded Auto-DL, built the Conventional Commits plugin for Jenkins, and enjoy mentoring new contributors and interns. Thanks for stopping by — feel free to reach out!
